- [Olson et al., Burning and Suppression of Solids–II Summary Report, NASA/TM-20210011385 (2021)](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20210011385). Appendix A supplies linked trial observations. Table 2.1 supplies the measured opposed-flow thin-PMMA extinction velocities. Section 2.3.7 defines the scope of the normal-gravity and ISS comparison. Figure 1.2 appears in the demo.
- [A Researcher's Guide to Combustion Science, NASA NP-2015-10-034-JSC](https://www.nasa.gov/sites/default/files/atoms/files/np-2015-10-034-jsc_combustion_in_reduced_gravity-iss-mini-book-011116-508.pdf). This supplies the second indexed document and historical context.
- [NASA PSI-25 recordings](https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo/data/investigations/PSI-25). The B1 recording supplies the demo cover frame. The dashboard links the original and derived playback files back to their sources in the [media catalog](https://github.com/DuarteFernandezPineiro/NASA-project/blob/main/flame-in-freefall/dist/media/catalog.json).
- [NASA Saffire experiment documentation](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20170000230), [NASA SoFIE](https://science.nasa.gov/biological-physical/investigations/sofie/), and [NASA exploration atmosphere concept](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20240003211). These inform study and exploration context. The catalog references the original PSI records for experimental values.

## External resources and tools

- [SDSU BASS experiment archive](http://flame.sdsu.edu/bass.html). Additional BASS-II views and still sequences used in the dashboard. See the media catalog for individual URLs.
- [OpenAI API](https://platform.openai.com/docs/overview) and [Codex](https://openai.com/codex/). Model generation, embeddings, development and presentation assistance.
- [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/), [Python](https://www.python.org/), [pdfplumber](https://github.com/jsvine/pdfplumber), and [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org/). Local server, CSV and PDF processing, and recorded-media conversion. Browser delivery uses HTML, CSS, JavaScript and SVG. [ChatGPT Sites](https://flame-in-freefall-bass-ii.knowingpanda2.chatgpt.site) serves the restricted live deployment.
- [Robertson and Zaragoza, BM25 and Beyond](https://doi.org/10.1561/1500000019) and [Cormack, Clarke and Büttcher, Reciprocal Rank Fusion](https://doi.org/10.1145/1571941.1572114). Retrieval methods used by the project.
- Screenshots in the deck are team captures of the deployed dashboard. Fonts are Bahnschrift, Segoe UI and Consolas in the deck, and Barlow Condensed, Instrument Sans and IBM Plex Mono in the web app.
- [2026 project submission guide](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/project-submission-guide/), [2026 judging and awards guide](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/judging-awards-guide/), and [official challenge](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/flame-in-freefall/).
